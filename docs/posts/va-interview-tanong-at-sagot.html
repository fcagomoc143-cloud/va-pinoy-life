Mga Karaniwang Tanong sa VA Interview at Paano Sumagot Nang Kumpiyansa

Mga tanong na madalas lumabas sa VA interview, kasama ang sample answers at tips para hindi ka kabahan sa harap ng client.

Congrats, may interview ka na! Ngayon, ang kaba. Normal lang yan. Kahit ako, noong unang interview ko sa foreign client, nanginginig ang boses ko.

Pero ang sikreto ay preparation. Kapag alam mo na ang mga posibleng tanong at may naisip ka nang sagot, mas kalmado ka. Heto ang mga madalas itanong at kung paano sumagot.

Bago ang Interview: Quick Prep

  • I-test ang internet, camera, at mic 30 minutes bago.
  • Mag-login nang 5 minutes mas maaga.
  • Magsuot ng maayos na damit, kahit polo o blouse lang.
  • I-double check ang time zone ng interview. Ito ang pinaka-madalas na pagkakamali.
  • Basahin ulit ang job post at ang resume mo.

Tanong 1: "Tell me about yourself."

Hindi ito tungkol sa buong buhay mo. Ito ay maikling pitch tungkol sa kung bakit ka bagay sa trabaho.

Formula: Sino ka ngayon, ano ang experience mo, at bakit ka interesado sa role na ito.

Sample: "I'm a Virtual Assistant based in the Philippines with experience in email management and scheduling. Before this, I worked in customer service, where I learned to stay organized and handle many tasks at once. I'm excited about this role because I enjoy helping busy business owners stay on top of their day."

Panatilihin itong mga 60 hanggang 90 segundo lang.

Tanong 2: "Why do you want to work as a VA?"

Iwasan ang "Kasi po work from home." Totoo man, mas maganda kung naka-focus sa value na maibibigay mo.

Sample: "I like supporting people behind the scenes and making their work easier. Being a VA lets me use my organization and communication skills while working with clients from different countries."

Tanong 3: "What tools are you familiar with?"

Maging honest. Ilista ang talagang alam mo, at banggitin kung paano mo ginamit.

Sample: "I use Google Workspace daily, especially Gmail, Calendar, and Sheets. I've also used Canva for social media graphics and Trello for tracking tasks. If you use a tool I haven't tried yet, I'm a quick learner and happy to learn it."

Tanong 4: "How do you handle multiple tasks with tight deadlines?"

Gusto nilang makita na may sistema ka.

Sample: "I list all my tasks first, then sort them by urgency and importance. If two urgent tasks clash, I'll ask the client which one to prioritize. I'd rather check than guess."

Tanong 5: "What would you do if your internet goes down?"

Pang-Pilipinas na tanong ito, at dapat may sagot ka agad.

Sample: "I have a backup pocket WiFi from a different provider and a UPS for my modem. If both fail, I'll message you right away through my phone and move to a nearby coworking space if needed."

Tanong 6: "What's your expected rate?"

Ito ang pinaka-nakakakaba. Mag-research muna ng typical rates para sa role na ina-applyan mo, dahil nag-iiba ito depende sa job at client.

Tips: - Magbigay ng range, hindi isang exact na number. - Huwag masyadong mababa para lang matanggap. Pwedeng maging mahirap itaas later. - Kung nasa job post na ang rate, okay lang sabihing "The rate you posted works for me."

Sample: "Based on my skills and the tasks in this role, I'm looking at a range of X to Y USD per hour. I'm open to discussing this based on the full scope of work."

Tanong 7: "Are you okay working night shift?"

Maging totoo. Kung kaya mo, sabihin. Kung may limitations ka, banggitin nang maayos.

Sample: "Yes, I'm comfortable working during your business hours. I've already adjusted my routine for it."

Mga Tanong na Pwede Mong Itanong sa Client

Sa dulo ng interview, madalas tatanungin ka: "Do you have any questions for me?" Laging may itanong. Ipinapakita nito na interesado ka.

Tanong Bakit Maganda
"What does a typical day look like in this role?" Malalaman mo ang tunay na tasks
"What tools does your team use?" Makakapag-prepare ka
"How do you prefer to communicate, email or chat?" Ipinapakita na iniisip mo ang workflow nila
"What would success look like after the first month?" Malalaman mo ang expectations
"What's the next step in the hiring process?" Malinaw kung ano ang susunod

Mga Dapat Iwasan

  1. Pagsasabi ng "Yes" sa lahat kahit hindi mo talaga alam. Mas okay ang "I haven't used that yet, but I can learn it quickly."
  2. Pagbabasa ng script. Halata ito. Gumamit ng bullet notes, hindi buong sentences.
  3. Pagrereklamo tungkol sa dating trabaho o boss. Red flag ito sa clients.
  4. Masyadong mahabang sagot. Diretso at malinaw.
  5. Pagkalimot mag-follow up. Magpadala ng maikling thank-you email pagkatapos.

Sample Thank-You Email

"Hi [Name], thank you for your time today. I enjoyed learning more about the role and your business. I'm excited about the chance to support you. Please let me know if you need anything else from me. Best regards, [Your Name]"

Simple, maikli, at professional.
