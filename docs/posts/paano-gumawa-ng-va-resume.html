Paano Gumawa ng VA Resume na Papansinin ng Client Abroad

Hindi tatalab ang lumang local resume sa client abroad. Gamitin ang libreng resume template habang binabasa ito.

🔄 Local resume vs VA resume

🗃️ Local (luma)

  • Birthday, edad, height, religion
  • Formal na 2x2 photo
  • Mahabang generic na "Objective"
  • Listahan ng duties
  • 3 pahina pataas
vs

✨ VA (bago)

  • Walang personal details na hindi related
  • Optional o simpleng headshot
  • Maikling "Summary" pang-VA
  • Results at tools na ginamit
  • 1 hanggang 2 pahina lang

🧱 Ang tamang structure

  1. 1🪪Header: pangalan, email, LinkedIn, portfolio
  2. 2💬Professional Summary: 2 hanggang 3 pangungusap
  3. 3🛠️Skills
  4. 4🧰Tools
  5. 5💼Work Experience, kahit hindi VA
  6. 6🎓Education at Training, kasama online courses
  7. 7🏠Work Setup: internet, backup, equipment

🏠 Madalas kinakalimutan ang Work Setup, pero malaking bagay ito sa clients.

✍️ Summary at results

😐 Mahina

  • "Hardworking and dedicated person..."
  • "Answered customer emails."
  • "Handled schedules."
vs

💪 Mas maganda

  • "Detail-oriented VA, email and calendar, Google Workspace"
  • "Responded to around 50 customer emails daily"
  • "Managed a 10-person team calendar, fewer double bookings"

🙏 Huwag mag-imbento ng numbers. Gumamit ng "around" kung hindi sigurado.

🧰 Walang experience? Ilagay ito

  • 🏪Previous jobsRetail, BPO, o opisina
  • 💸RaketOnline selling, tutoring, events
  • 🤝VolunteerOrg experience
  • 🗂️ProjectsSample works mula sa portfolio
  • 💻ToolsGoogle Workspace, Slack, Zoom, Trello, Asana, Canva

🎨 Formatting at final checklist

  • Malinis na font: Arial, Calibri, o Inter
  • Naka-PDF, filename tulad ng Juan-Dela-Cruz-VA-Resume.pdf
  • Walang typo, ipabasa o gumamit ng grammar checker
  • Gumagana ang LinkedIn at portfolio links
  • 1 hanggang 2 pahina lang
  • Naka-customize ang summary sa job
  • Tools na talagang alam mo lang
  • Tinanggal ang edad, religion, at iba pang personal details
