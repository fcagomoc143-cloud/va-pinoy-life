Paano Makipag-usap sa Boss na Taga-Abroad: Culture at Email Tone

Madalas hindi skills ang problema kundi communication at culture.

Pinoy habit vs mas magandang gawin

Pinoy habit

  • Laging "Yes" kahit hindi sigurado
  • Tahimik kapag may problema
  • Sobrang "Sorry"
  • Laging "Sir" o "Ma'am"
  • Naghihintay ng utos
vs

Mas magandang gawin

  • Sabihin kung may hindi naintindihan
  • I-report agad kasama ang solusyon
  • Isang sorry lang, tapos solusyon
  • First name kung yun ang gusto nila
  • Mag-suggest ng next steps

Hindi sigurado sa tawag? Itanong: "How would you like me to address you?"

5-step email structure

  1. 1Greeting: "Hi [Name],"
  2. 2Purpose agad sa unang pangungusap
  3. 3Details, bullets kung marami
  4. 4Action o tanong
  5. 5Closing: "Thanks," o "Best,"

Before vs after

Before

  • "Good day Sir! So sorry to disturb you..."
  • "...if it is okay if I will move the meeting..."
  • "Sorry again Sir."
vs

After

  • "Hi [Name], your 2 PM Thursday meeting conflicts with the vendor call."
  • "Move the vendor call to Friday 10 AM?"
  • "I'll update both invites once you confirm. Thanks!"

Mga phrases na malaking tulong

Sitwasyon Gamitin ito
Hindi naintindihan "Just to make sure I understand, you'd like me to..."
May problema "Quick heads-up: X happened. Here's what I suggest..."
Kailangan ng oras "I need a bit more time. Can I send it by [time]?"
Mag-suggest "One idea: we could try X. Happy to go with what you prefer."
Tapos na "Done! Here's the link. Let me know if you'd like changes."

Bad news rule

  1. 1Ano ang nangyari
  2. 2Ano ang epekto
  3. 3Ano ang plano mo

Magsabi agad. Mas masama ang dating kapag huli nilang nalaman ang problema.

Chat, email, at culture tips

  • ChatCasual, maikli, emoji kung ginagamit ng team
  • EmailMas formal, kumpleto, walang emoji
  • Huwag "Hi" langIsulat agad ang buong tanong
  • Oras nilaWalang message sa weekend o gabi nila
  • Small talkOkay, pero hindi masyadong personal
  • HolidaysAlamin ang major holidays ng client
