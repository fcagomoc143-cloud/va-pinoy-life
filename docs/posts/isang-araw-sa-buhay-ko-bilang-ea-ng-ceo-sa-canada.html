Isang Araw sa Buhay Ko Bilang EA ng CEO sa Canada

Ganito ang typical na araw ng isang remote EA. Generic ang detalye, pero totoo ang daloy.

Timezone reality

  • 12 hanggang 16 hrsKaraniwang diperensya ng Canada at PH
  • GabiAng "umaga" ng client ay gabi natin

Sample na schedule (oras sa PH)

  1. 13 PM: gising, kape, konting exercise
  2. 25 PM: inbox at calendar triage
  3. 36 PM: prep ng daily brief
  4. 48 PM: overlap hours, meetings at requests
  5. 512 AM: break at hapunan
  6. 61 AM: follow-ups, documents, travel
  7. 73 AM: end-of-day report at planning
  8. 84 AM: log off, wind down

Inbox triage bago magsimula ang client

  • SortUrgent, review, FYI, kaya kong sagutin
  • SumagotRoutine emails gamit ang templates
  • Mag-flagKailangan ng desisyon niya
  • Calendar checkConflicts, links, kulang na prep

Ang daily brief

  • Meetings ngayong araw at kailangang prep
  • Top emails na kailangan ng sagot
  • Paparating na deadlines
  • Follow-ups na hinihintay pa

Sikreto sa overlap hours: may template, checklist, at folder system para sa lahat.

Karaniwang tools ng EA

  • Email at calendarCore ng trabaho
  • Video conferencingMeetings at notes
  • Task trackerProjects at follow-ups
  • Cloud storageDocuments
  • Password managerSecure na access
  • Note-taking appMeeting notes

Ang hindi nakikita ng iba

  • DiscretionSensitive info, hindi pinag-uusapan
  • AnticipationAlam na bago pa hingin
  • BoundariesMay oras para sa sarili at pamilya
