Google Workspace Basics na Dapat Alam ng Bawat VA

Marunong ka sa Gmail? Kalahati na ng Google Workspace ang alam mo.

🏢 Free Gmail vs Workspace

🙂 Free Gmail

  • name@gmail.com
  • Walang admin
  • Basic storage
  • Simpleng sharing
vs

🏢 Google Workspace

  • name@company.com
  • May admin controls
  • Mas malaki, depende sa plan
  • Mas maraming security settings

🔗 Client ang nagbibigay ng account. Check official site for pricing: workspace.google.com

🧩 Ang core apps

  • 📧GmailLabels, filters, templates
  • 📅CalendarFind a time, time zones
  • 📁DriveShared drives, permissions
  • 📝DocsSuggesting mode, comments
  • 📊SheetsFreeze rows, dropdowns, SUM, XLOOKUP
  • 🎥Meet at FormsCalls at forms papuntang Sheets

📧 Gmail at Calendar power moves

  • Labels**: pwedeng dalawang label ang isang email
  • Filters**: auto-label at skip inbox
  • Templates**: i-enable sa settings para sa paulit-ulit na reply
  • Delegation**: access sa inbox ng boss nang walang password
  • Secondary time zone para kita ang oras mo at ng boss
  • Laging may Meet link o location ang invite

📁 Drive sharing levels

  • ViewerBasa lang
  • CommenterBasa at comment
  • EditorEdit at minsan share

⚠️ Iwasan ang "Anyone with the link" sa sensitive files. I-share sa specific na tao lang.

🗓️ 7-day practice plan

  1. 1🏷️Day 1: 5 labels at 2 filters
  2. 2✉️Day 2: 3 email templates
  3. 3🌏Day 3: Calendar na may secondary time zone
  4. 4📁Day 4: Ayusin ang Drive folders
  5. 5📝Day 5: SOP sa Docs, Suggesting mode
  6. 6📊Day 6: Expense tracker na may dropdown at SUM
  7. 7📋Day 7: Google Form na konektado sa Sheet

🚫 Common na pagkakamali

  • 👤Maling recipientI-double check ang email address
  • 💾Offline editingNawawala ang version history
  • 🗑️Delete sa shared driveBaka file ng iba yan
  • 🕐Maling time zoneI-check bago mag-send ng invite
