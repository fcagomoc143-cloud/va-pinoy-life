Ganito ang typical na araw ng isang remote EA. Generic ang detalye, pero **totoo ang daloy**.

## 🌏 Timezone reality

:::stats
12 hanggang 16 hrs | Karaniwang diperensya ng Canada at PH
Gabi | Ang "umaga" ng client ay gabi natin
:::

## ⏰ Sample na schedule (oras sa PH)

:::steps
☕ | 3 PM: gising, kape, konting exercise
📥 | 5 PM: inbox at calendar triage
🗞️ | 6 PM: prep ng daily brief
💬 | 8 PM: overlap hours, meetings at requests
🍜 | 12 AM: break at hapunan
✈️ | 1 AM: follow-ups, documents, travel
📝 | 3 AM: end-of-day report at planning
😴 | 4 AM: log off, wind down
:::

## 📥 Inbox triage bago magsimula ang client

:::cards
🗂️ | Sort | Urgent, review, FYI, kaya kong sagutin
✉️ | Sumagot | Routine emails gamit ang templates
🚩 | Mag-flag | Kailangan ng desisyon niya
📅 | Calendar check | Conflicts, links, kulang na prep
:::

## 🗞️ Ang daily brief

:::check
Meetings ngayong araw at kailangang prep
Top emails na kailangan ng sagot
Paparating na deadlines
Follow-ups na hinihintay pa
:::

:::tip
⚙️ Sikreto sa overlap hours: may template, checklist, at folder system para sa lahat.
:::

## 🧰 Karaniwang tools ng EA

:::cards
📧 | Email at calendar | Core ng trabaho
🎥 | Video conferencing | Meetings at notes
✅ | Task tracker | Projects at follow-ups
☁️ | Cloud storage | Documents
🔐 | Password manager | Secure na access
🗒️ | Note-taking app | Meeting notes
:::

## 👀 Ang hindi nakikita ng iba

:::cards
🤫 | Discretion | Sensitive info, hindi pinag-uusapan
🔮 | Anticipation | Alam na bago pa hingin
🚧 | Boundaries | May oras para sa sarili at pamilya
:::
