Ang AI ay parang junior assistant: sobrang bilis, pero kailangang bantayan.

## 🤖 Ang tatlong sikat na AI tools

:::cards
💬 | [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) | OpenAI, versatile, maraming integrations
📄 | [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Anthropic, mahahabang docs, natural na sulat
🔷 | [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) | Google, konektado sa Workspace
🪟 | Copilot | Built-in sa Microsoft 365, depende sa plan
:::

:::tip
💸 Mabilis magbago ang features at limits. Check official site for pricing, at tanungin ang client kung alin ang approved.
:::

## ⚡ Saan ko ginagamit ang AI

:::cards
✉️ | Email drafts | Main points mo, draft ng AI
📚 | Summaries | Public reports, bullet points lang
🗓️ | Meeting agenda | Topic at attendees, structured agenda
✍️ | English polish | Grammar at tone check
💡 | Brainstorming | Event, gift, at title ideas
📋 | SOP drafts | Steps mo, format ng AI
:::

## 🔒 Privacy rules, non-negotiable

:::steps
🙋 | Tanungin muna ang client kung pwede
🚫 | Huwag mag-paste ng sensitive data
🎭 | I-anonymize: "Client A", "X amount"
⚙️ | I-check ang data at training settings
🏢 | Gamitin ang approved na company account
:::

:::check
🚫 Passwords, account numbers, credit card details
🚫 Address, phone, ID numbers ng tao
🚫 Financial records ng kumpanya
🚫 Contracts at legal documents
🚫 Health information
🚫 Anumang may label na confidential
:::

## ⚠️ Nagkakamali ang AI

:::vs
❌ Huwag | ✅ Gawin
I-send nang hindi binabasa | Basahin at i-edit bawat draft
Maniwala agad sa facts | I-verify ang dates, presyo, lugar
Hayaang tunog-robot | Gawing tunog-tao at tunog-boss
Legal, medical, tax advice | Ipasa sa tamang expert
:::

:::tip
🧠 Ikaw pa rin ang responsable sa output. Hindi pwedeng sisihin ang AI.
:::

## 🧪 Prompt formula

:::steps
🎭 | Role: "Act as an executive assistant"
🎯 | Task: "Draft an email to a vendor"
📌 | Context: "Delivery is one week late"
🎚️ | Tone: "Firm but polite"
📐 | Format: "Max 5 sentences, may subject line"
:::

## ✅ Safety check bago mag-paste

:::check
Approved ba ng client ang tool na ito?
Walang personal o confidential info?
Na-anonymize na ang names at numbers?
Tamang account ang gamit ko?
Babasahin at ie-edit ko ang output?
Na-verify ko na ang facts?
:::

:::tip
🛑 May "hindi" o "hindi sigurado"? Huminto muna.
:::

## 👉 Next step

:::tip
🎯 Tanungin ang client: "Anong AI tools ang approved para sa trabaho ko, at anong data ang bawal?"
