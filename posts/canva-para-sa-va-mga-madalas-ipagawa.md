Hindi mo kailangang maging designer. Kailangan mo lang ng Canva at ilang rules.

## 💛 Bakit Canva ang gusto ng clients

:::cards
🖱️ | Madaling gamitin | Drag and drop
📐 | Maraming templates | Hindi magsisimula sa wala
🌐 | Browser-based | Walang i-install
🤝 | Collaboration | Sabay mag-edit ang boss at ikaw
🆓 | May free plan | Mas marami sa Pro at Teams
:::

:::tip
💸 Check official site for pricing: [canva.com/pricing](https://www.canva.com/pricing)
:::

## 📋 Madalas ipagawa sa VA

:::cards
📱 | Social posts | Consistent na fonts at colors
🖥️ | Slides 16:9 | Isang idea bawat slide
📄 | Flyer o poster | Malaking headline, kaunting text
🏅 | Certificates | Gamitin ang Bulk Create
✉️ | Email headers | I-check ang tamang sukat
🎬 | Thumbnails | Malaking text, mataas na contrast
:::

:::tip
📏 Sa North America, **Letter size** ang standard sa printing, hindi A4.
:::

## 🎨 Design rules para hindi tunog-baguhan

:::stats
2 | Fonts max
3 | Colors lang
1 | Idea bawat slide
100% | Aligned gamit ang guides
:::

:::check
Brand Kit: logo, hex colors, fonts (paid plan)
Free plan? Gumawa ng sariling "brand guide" design
White space ay hindi sayang
Pinakamalaki ang date at title sa flyer
:::

## 🔁 Workflow sa bawat request

:::steps
❓ | Linawin: para saan, size, deadline
🔍 | Humingi ng reference na style
📐 | Pumili ng template
🎨 | I-apply ang brand
✏️ | Ilagay ang content
🔎 | I-proofread: spelling, dates, links
💬 | I-share para sa feedback
⬇️ | I-download sa tamang format
:::

## 💾 Tamang file format

:::vs
📤 Format | 🎯 Kailan
PNG | Social media, sharp na text
JPG | Photos, maliit na file
PDF Standard | Email o screen
PDF Print | Printing, hindi JPG
MP4 | Animated o video
PPTX | Ie-edit pa sa PowerPoint
:::

## ⏱️ Time-savers at copyright

:::cards
📐 | Resize | Isang design, maraming sizes
🏅 | Bulk Create | Maraming pangalan sa certificates
📁 | Templates folder | Para sa recurring tasks
⌨️ | Shortcuts | T para text, Ctrl + D duplicate
©️ | License | Basahin ang [content license](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement)
:::
