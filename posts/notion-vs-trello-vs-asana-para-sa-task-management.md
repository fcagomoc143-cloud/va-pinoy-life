Alin ang pinakamaganda? Depende. Eto kung kailan bagay ang bawat isa.

## 🧩 Quick overview

:::cards
📌 | [Trello](https://trello.com) | Kanban boards, sticky notes style
🎯 | [Asana](https://asana.com) | Structured na project management
🧱 | [Notion](https://www.notion.so) | All-in-one, parang LEGO
:::

## ⚖️ Side-by-side

| Feature | Trello | Asana | Notion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bagay sa | Simpleng workflows | Team projects na may deadlines | Docs at tasks sa iisang lugar |
| Learning curve | Mababa | Katamtaman | Katamtaman hanggang mataas |
| Main view | Kanban boards | List, board, timeline, calendar | Databases, maraming views |
| Docs at SOPs | Limitado | Limitado | Malakas |
| Team collaboration | Maganda | Napakaganda | Maganda |
| Free plan at mobile app | Meron | Meron | Meron |

:::tip
💸 May limits ang free plans. Check official site for pricing ng bawat isa.
:::

## 📊 Gaano kabilis matutunan

:::bars
Trello | 90 | 15 minuto, gamit mo na
Asana | 60 | Mas maraming features
Notion | 45 | Kailangan ng setup at structure
:::

## 👍 Lakas at 👎 kahinaan

:::vs
👍 Lakas | 👎 Kahinaan
Trello: sobrang visual at simple | Trello: gumugulo pag lumaki
Asana: malinaw kung sino at kailan | Asana: timeline at reports nasa paid
Asana: malakas sa recurring tasks | Asana: mas matagal matutunan
Notion: SOPs, notes, tasks sabay | Notion: madaling maging magulo
Notion: maraming libreng templates | Notion: kulang minsan sa malaking team
:::
