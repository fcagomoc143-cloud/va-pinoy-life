Ang sikreto ng maayos na biyahe ng boss ay hindi swerte. **System** ito.

## 🧳 Traveler profile

:::cards
✈️ | Airlines | Preferred at loyalty programs
💺 | Seat at cabin | Aisle o window, approved na class
🏨 | Hotels | Preferred chains at room preferences
🍽️ | Diet | Dietary restrictions
🚕 | Ground transport | Taxi, rideshare, o car rental
⏰ | Airport timing | Gaano kaaga gusto niya
:::

:::tip
🔐 Passport, birthdate, at loyalty numbers ay sensitive. Sa password manager o secure na profile lang.
:::

## 🔎 Saan mag-book

:::cards
🏨 | Direct sa airline o hotel | Mas madaling mag-change o cancel
🔍 | [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights) | Pang-compare, booking sa airline
🏢 | Corporate platform | Sundin ang travel policy
🧑‍💼 | Travel agent | Complex na multi-city, kung approved
:::

:::tip
💸 Nagbabago ang presyo araw-araw. I-confirm bago mag-book at kunin ang approval kung lagpas sa budget.
:::

## 🔁 Booking process

:::steps
📝 | Kunin ang destination, dates, purpose
🛂 | I-check ang passport at visa sa official government site
🔍 | 3 flight at 2 o 3 hotel options
🙋 | Ipapili sa boss, maikli lang
✅ | Mag-book pagkatapos ng approval
📁 | I-save ang confirmations sa iisang folder
📅 | Ilagay sa calendar, may confirmation numbers
🗺️ | Gumawa ng itinerary
📲 | Online check-in pag bukas na
:::

## 🗺️ Itinerary sample, Day 1

:::stats
6:00 AM | Pick up, rideshare
8:30 AM | Flight, number at terminal
11:45 AM | Arrival, local time
1:00 PM | Hotel check-in, confirmation no.
3:00 PM | Meeting, address at dress code
7:00 PM | Dinner reservation
:::

:::check
Hotel phone at emergency contacts
Local time zone at agwat sa home
Weather forecast
Plug type at currency kung international
:::

## 🆘 Pag may aberya

:::steps
😌 | Huwag mag-panic
🔍 | I-check agad ang susunod na flights o hotels
📞 | Kontakin ang airline o hotel
💬 | I-update ang boss na may options, hindi lang problema
📅 | I-adjust ang calendar at abisuhan ang meetings
:::

## ✅ Pre-trip checklist

:::check
Confirmed ang flights
Hotel para sa lahat ng gabi
Passport validity at visa requirements
Hindi masyadong maikli ang layover
Ground transport pagdating at pag-alis
Travel insurance kung required
Calendar na may local time
Naipadala na ang itinerary
Na-reschedule ang tatamaang meetings
:::
