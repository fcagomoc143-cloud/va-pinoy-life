Madalas hindi skills ang problema kundi **communication** at culture.

## 🌏 Pinoy habit vs mas magandang gawin

:::vs
🇵🇭 Pinoy habit | ✅ Mas magandang gawin
Laging "Yes" kahit hindi sigurado | Sabihin kung may hindi naintindihan
Tahimik kapag may problema | I-report agad kasama ang solusyon
Sobrang "Sorry" | Isang sorry lang, tapos solusyon
Laging "Sir" o "Ma'am" | First name kung yun ang gusto nila
Naghihintay ng utos | Mag-suggest ng next steps
:::

:::tip
🙋 Hindi sigurado sa tawag? Itanong: "How would you like me to address you?"
:::

## ✉️ 5-step email structure

:::steps
👋 | Greeting: "Hi [Name],"
🎯 | Purpose agad sa unang pangungusap
📌 | Details, bullets kung marami
❓ | Action o tanong
✍️ | Closing: "Thanks," o "Best,"
:::

## 🔄 Before vs after

:::vs
😬 Before | 😎 After
"Good day Sir! So sorry to disturb you..." | "Hi [Name], your 2 PM Thursday meeting conflicts with the vendor call."
"...if it is okay if I will move the meeting..." | "Move the vendor call to Friday 10 AM?"
"Sorry again Sir." | "I'll update both invites once you confirm. Thanks!"
:::

## 🗣️ Mga phrases na malaking tulong

| Sitwasyon | Gamitin ito |
|---|---|
| Hindi naintindihan | "Just to make sure I understand, you'd like me to..." |
| May problema | "Quick heads-up: X happened. Here's what I suggest..." |
| Kailangan ng oras | "I need a bit more time. Can I send it by [time]?" |
| Mag-suggest | "One idea: we could try X. Happy to go with what you prefer." |
| Tapos na | "Done! Here's the link. Let me know if you'd like changes." |

## 🚨 Bad news rule

:::steps
❗ | Ano ang nangyari
📉 | Ano ang epekto
🛠️ | Ano ang plano mo
:::

:::tip
⏰ Magsabi agad. Mas masama ang dating kapag huli nilang nalaman ang problema.
:::

## 🌐 Chat, email, at culture tips

:::cards
💬 | Chat | Casual, maikli, emoji kung ginagamit ng team
📧 | Email | Mas formal, kumpleto, walang emoji
🚫 | Huwag "Hi" lang | Isulat agad ang buong tanong
🕰️ | Oras nila | Walang message sa weekend o gabi nila
☕ | Small talk | Okay, pero hindi masyadong personal
🎉 | Holidays | Alamin ang major holidays ng client
:::
