Marunong ka sa Gmail? Kalahati na ng Google Workspace ang alam mo.

## 🏢 Free Gmail vs Workspace

:::vs
🙂 Free Gmail | 🏢 Google Workspace
name@gmail.com | name@company.com
Walang admin | May admin controls
Basic storage | Mas malaki, depende sa plan
Simpleng sharing | Mas maraming security settings
:::

:::tip
🔗 Client ang nagbibigay ng account. Check official site for pricing: [workspace.google.com](https://workspace.google.com)
:::

## 🧩 Ang core apps

:::cards
📧 | Gmail | Labels, filters, templates
📅 | Calendar | Find a time, time zones
📁 | Drive | Shared drives, permissions
📝 | Docs | Suggesting mode, comments
📊 | Sheets | Freeze rows, dropdowns, SUM, XLOOKUP
🎥 | Meet at Forms | Calls at forms papuntang Sheets
:::

## 📧 Gmail at Calendar power moves

:::check
**Labels**: pwedeng dalawang label ang isang email
**Filters**: auto-label at skip inbox
**Templates**: i-enable sa settings para sa paulit-ulit na reply
**Delegation**: access sa inbox ng boss nang walang password
Secondary time zone para kita ang oras mo at ng boss
Laging may Meet link o location ang invite
:::

## 📁 Drive sharing levels

:::bars
Viewer | 33 | Basa lang
Commenter | 66 | Basa at comment
Editor | 100 | Edit at minsan share
:::

:::tip
⚠️ Iwasan ang "Anyone with the link" sa sensitive files. I-share sa specific na tao lang.
:::

:::steps

:::

## 🚫 Common na pagkakamali

:::cards
👤 | Maling recipient | I-double check ang email address
💾 | Offline editing | Nawawala ang version history
🗑️ | Delete sa shared drive | Baka file ng iba yan
🕐 | Maling time zone | I-check bago mag-send ng invite
:::
